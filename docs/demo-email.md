What it does

Your assistants place an appraisal order on one page and attach the contract. I get notified and accept it. The borrower (or the agent, when access goes through the agent) gets a text and an email with a personal link and books the inspection against my real calendar, with no account to create. Everyone at the bank sees the same live status bar, from Received through Scheduled, Inspected, Delivered and Invoiced. I deliver the report and the invoice through the portal instead of email, the loan officer downloads them, and the borrower gets their copy through the same link. Every step, message, document and download is written to an independence record you can export for an examiner. The back and forth email goes away.

Try it in five minutes

1. Open the link and choose Appraisal desk. You are Maria, a loan officer assistant. The board shows eight sample orders. Click 812 N Roosevelt Ave to see an order that is waiting on the borrower to pick a time.
2. Click New order, type an address and a borrower, tick the attestation, and place it. Then attach any PDF on the Documents tab.
3. Sign out (top right) and choose Appraiser. Open 1420 Sycamore Ln and click Accept, then Send scheduling request. On the Messages tab you will see the exact text and email the borrower gets. Open the link in that text on your phone: that is the borrower's page. Pick a time. Back at the computer, the order moves to Scheduled on its own.
4. Choose Loan officer to see the read-only view and download the sample report on 2201 Ironwood Ct. Choose Bank administrator to see how the bank adds people and sets what each one can do.
5. The Outbox tab shows every text and email the portal writes, word for word, so you can tell me what to change.

Why it is easy to live with

There is nothing for the bank to install or maintain. It runs in any browser and on phones, borrowers never create accounts, and your administrator adds or removes staff in a few clicks. It is hosted on Cloudflare's network with encrypted connections, backups that can restore any point in the last thirty days, and automatic updates that I handle. The running cost is a few dollars a month for hosting and email. I will put the arrangement in writing once you have had a chance to try it.

The bank controls access. Each person has their own login and a role: assistants place orders, loan officers can only view and download, the appraiser can only move the file forward. That separation is what keeps the record clean for an exam.
