# FSB Appraisal Desk: administrator guide

For the person First Security Bank has designated to administer the portal at https://fsb.apprifi.com.

## Your first sign-in

You will receive a one-time invitation link from the vendor. Open it, choose a password of at least ten characters, and you are signed in as the bank administrator. The link stops working after that. If it expires before you use it (seven days), ask the vendor for another.

## Adding people

Open the People tab and choose Add someone. Enter their name, work email and role:

Appraisal desk is the loan officer assistant who places orders, uploads the sales contract and other documents, edits or cancels orders, and sends factual questions to the appraiser. Loan officer sees status and downloads the finished report and invoice, and nothing else; this keeps the independence record clean. Appraiser accepts or declines orders, sends scheduling requests, logs the inspection, delivers the report and invoice, and sets their own availability. Lender admin can do what you can do.

Each new person gets a one-time sign-in link that lasts seven days. When email is switched on it is sent to them the moment you save; it is also shown to you, so you can copy it or use Send by email if they need it another way. When someone forgets their password they can use Forgot your password? on the sign-in page and receive a two-hour reset link by email; the New sign-in link button on their row does the same by hand, and the old password stops working once they use either. Suspend ends a person's access immediately and signs them out everywhere; Restore brings them back. A role can be changed from the drop-down on their row and takes effect at their next sign-in. You cannot suspend yourself or remove the last administrator.

## Lender settings

Lender branding sets the name, tagline, colors and logo that staff, borrowers and agents see; every message goes out in that name. Delivery and notifications shows whether email and texting are sending automatically or by hand, and has Send me a test email and Send me a test text buttons that send to your own address and mobile so you can see exactly what a borrower receives. Desk notices (accepted, scheduled, inspected, delivered, invoiced, holds and declines) always go to the person who placed the order; add shared or manager addresses there to copy them on every desk notice.

## Notices and the Outbox

Once email is switched on, every notice goes out within seconds and the Outbox shows Sent with the time; texts also show the carrier's delivery report. Until then, every email the portal composes waits in the Outbox with an Open in email app button that drafts it in Outlook or Gmail; the sender presses send there and then marks it sent, which records who sent it. Texts wait for carrier registration and have an Open in Messages button for a phone. A borrower who replies STOP to a text is opted out automatically and later texts to that number are held (the email still goes). Texts and emails that come back from a number or address the portal cannot match to an open order wait at the bottom of the Outbox screen.

## Conversation

Every order has a Conversation tab. The desk posts factual questions or additional property information to the appraiser; the appraiser answers there and can also send a note to the borrower or agent, which reaches them as a text and an email. The borrower or agent can reply from their status page, by text, or by replying to the email, and it lands on the same tab with the appraiser and desk notified. Loan officers can read the conversation but not take part. Unread entries are flagged on the order board. Everything said is on the record. Value is never discussed there.

## Records

Every order has a Record tab: who ordered it and under what role, the recusal attestation with its timestamp, every status change, message, document, and each time the borrower opened their copy. Export writes it as a text file to keep with the loan file. The People tab shows an administration record of every access change.

## What borrowers and agents see

They never sign in. Each receives a personal link in the text and email the appraiser triggers. The page shows the property, a plain-language status list, the booking calendar when it is time to schedule, an add-to-calendar file once booked, a way to change the time, a place to upload documents the appraiser asks for, a message box that reaches the appraiser and the desk, and, after delivery, a consent to electronic delivery followed by the report download. The link stops working if the desk reissues it, and reaches nothing but that one order.

## If something goes wrong

Use the red Feedback button on any screen; it records which screen you were on. For access problems (a locked-out administrator, a lost invitation) contact the vendor, who can reissue the administrator's invitation but cannot see or change anything else without an account you grant.
