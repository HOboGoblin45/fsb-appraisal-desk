# FSB Appraisal Desk: administrator guide

For the person First Security Bank has designated to administer the portal at https://fsb.apprifi.com.

## Your first sign-in

You will receive a one-time invitation link from the vendor. Open it, choose a password of at least ten characters, and you are signed in as the bank administrator. The link stops working after that. If it expires before you use it (seven days), ask the vendor for another.

## Adding people

Open the People tab and choose Add someone. Enter their name, work email and role:

Appraisal desk is the loan officer assistant who places orders, uploads the sales contract and other documents, edits or cancels orders, and sends factual questions to the appraiser. Loan officer sees status and downloads the finished report and invoice, and nothing else; this keeps the independence record clean. Appraiser accepts or declines orders, sends scheduling requests, logs the inspection, delivers the report and invoice, and sets their own availability. Bank admin can do what you can do.

Each new person gets a one-time sign-in link that lasts seven days. Copy it and send it to them, or use Send by email to draft the message in your mail program. When someone forgets their password, the New sign-in link button on their row issues a fresh one and the old password stops working once they use it. Suspend ends a person's access immediately and signs them out everywhere; Restore brings them back. A role can be changed from the drop-down on their row and takes effect at their next sign-in. You cannot suspend yourself or remove the last administrator.

## Bank settings

Desk notices (accepted, scheduled, inspected, delivered, invoiced, holds and declines) always go to the person who placed the order. Add shared or manager addresses under Bank settings to copy them on every desk notice. The same panel shows whether email and texting are sending automatically or by hand.

## Messages

Until automatic email is switched on, every email the portal composes waits in the Outbox with an Open in email app button that drafts it in Outlook or Gmail; the sender presses send there and then marks it sent, which records who sent it. Texts wait for carrier registration and have an Open in Messages button for a phone. Once email is switched on, messages go out within seconds and the Outbox shows Sent with the time.

## Records

Every order has a Record tab: who ordered it and under what role, the recusal attestation with its timestamp, every status change, message, document, and each time the borrower opened their copy. Export writes it as a text file to keep with the loan file. The People tab shows an administration record of every access change.

## What borrowers and agents see

They never sign in. Each receives a personal link in the text and email the appraiser triggers. The page shows the property, a plain-language status list, the booking calendar when it is time to schedule, an add-to-calendar file once booked, a way to change the time, and, after delivery, a consent to electronic delivery followed by the report download. The link stops working if the desk reissues it, and reaches nothing but that one order.

## If something goes wrong

Use the red Feedback button on any screen; it records which screen you were on. For access problems (a locked-out administrator, a lost invitation) contact the vendor, who can reissue the administrator's invitation but cannot see or change anything else without an account you grant.
