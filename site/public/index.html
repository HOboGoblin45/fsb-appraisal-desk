How it works

One order, six steps, no email threads.

1

The desk places the order

Property, loan, product, borrower and agent contacts, sales contract. The person ordering attests to recusal from the credit decision, on the record.

2

The appraiser accepts

The appraiser is emailed, accepts or declines in the portal, and the borrower is told an appraisal has been ordered, with a personal status link.

3

The borrower books the inspection

A text and an email carry a link to open times drawn from the appraiser's own working days, hours, travel buffer and days off. Confirmed in one tap, with a calendar file.

4

Everyone watches the same status bar

Inspected, in review, delivered. Questions and answers go on a per-order conversation that is logged, and the borrower can reply from their status page, by text or by email.

5

Report and invoice come through the portal

The appraiser uploads the report and invoice. The desk and loan officer download them; the borrower gets a copy after agreeing to electronic delivery. The desk records payment.

6

The record is already written

Every action above is on the order's independence record with who did it and when. Export it as a text file and keep it with the loan file.

The order

Status, documents, conversation and record on one screen

The desk sees what it is waiting on, the appraiser sees what to do next, the loan officer sees where things stand. Reviewer routing, preliminary figures for closing, revision requests and payment are all built in.

The borrower

One page, no sign-in, in the lender's name

Plain-language status, the booking calendar when it is time, documents the appraiser asks for, a message box, and the finished report once the lender delivers it. The link reaches nothing but that one order.

Your appraisers, your fees

Compliance

Built for the rules you already work under.

For lenders, appraiser independence is not a report you write after the fact. Apprifi enforces it in the workflow and records it as it happens. Firms get the same record without the lending-specific attestation.

Recusal at the point of order

Whoever places the order attests that they will abstain from the decision to approve, not approve, or set the terms of the transaction. The attestation, the person and the timestamp are on the record (12 CFR 1026.42(d)(3)(ii); Interagency Appraisal and Evaluation Guidelines).

Loan officers read, they do not touch

Loan production staff see status and download the finished report. They cannot place, edit or cancel an order, and they cannot take part in the conversation with the appraiser.

Direct engagement, on every record

Each order's record states that the lender engaged the appraiser directly and that no management company selected the appraiser, set the fee or reviewed the report.

Borrower copy with consent

The borrower receives a copy of the appraisal through their own link after agreeing to electronic delivery, and can ask for paper at no charge. The delivery and the consent are logged.

Every download logged

Files are served only as attachments, in a sandbox, to signed-in staff or to the borrower's own link after delivery. Downloads of the other side's documents go on the record.

Your data, your database

Each institution runs on its own database and document store on Cloudflare, under its own hostname, with roles assigned by its own administrator. The vendor holds no account inside your portal.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is Apprifi an appraisal management company?

No. Apprifi never selects the appraiser, sets or collects the fee, or reviews the report. Your institution engages its own appraisers directly. Apprifi is the software that coordinates and records the work, and every order's record says so.

Do borrowers or agents need an account?

No. Each receives a personal link by text and email that opens one page for one order: status, the booking calendar when it is time, documents the appraiser asks for, a message box, and the finished report after delivery and consent.

What does our appraiser need?

A browser and an invitation from your administrator. The appraiser sets working days, hours, inspection length, travel buffer and days off once; the portal offers borrowers only times that fit.

Who controls access?

Your institution. You name the administrator; Apprifi issues that one invitation and holds no account inside your portal afterwards. The administrator invites staff and appraisers, assigns roles, suspends accounts and issues sign-in links.

Where is our data?

On Cloudflare's platform, in a database and document store created for your institution alone, under your own hostname. Sessions are cookie based and expire; passwords are salted, peppered and hashed; every file is content checked and served only as an attachment to people entitled to it. Details are on the security and privacy page.

We are a law firm, not a lender. Does it fit?

Yes. A firm's portal uses its own titles (Paralegal, Attorney, Firm administrator), asks for a matter number instead of a loan number, skips the lender-only recusal attestation, and offers estate, trust, gift, partition and tax-appeal purposes with date-of-death and retrospective products. Executors, heirs and property managers get the same personal link a borrower would.

Can we keep our own branding?

Yes. Your name and tagline appear on every screen and message. Your administrator can add your logo and colors, or leave the Apprifi look.

How long does it take to start?

The portal is stood up for you in an afternoon. Your administrator's first sign-in, inviting people and the first order usually happen the same day. Carrier registration for text messaging takes longer and is arranged during setup; email works from day one.
